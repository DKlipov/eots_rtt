Empire of the Sun – Expert Mode

This mode automates many player actions to expedite asynchronous games. Players who are less familiar with the rules of the game may find it difficult to follow the flow of the game when these steps are automated, so it is recommended only for expert players who are already familiar with the rules. Automated actions can still be reviewed in the game log.

  • Special reaction rolls automated.
  • Reaction steps reduced when it is obvious that the Reaction player has no available cards for use.
  • Battle hexes checked automatically from A to Z, one by one.
  • Loss applied automatically if only one possible unit to assign hit (could be repeated multiple times), or all units could be eliminated.
  • Some reaction cards applied automatically, hits applied automatically if only one unit possible.
  • Attrition loss automated.
  • Submarine warfare rolls automated.
