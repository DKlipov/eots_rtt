Small introductory and tournament scenario, uses the South Pacific mini-map, four game turns long.

The scenario focuses on the Allied drives up the Solomon Islands and New Guinea. The game features a damaged but still powerful Imperial Japanese Navy (IJN) poised to slow and potentially stop the Allied advance. The game starts on the eve of Operation Watchtower (Guadalcanal Invasion), so this card begins in the opening Allied hand of cards. The Japanese hand begins with the Savo Island reaction card setting up the potential for the historical opening.

Victory is determined by the VP schedule outlined in the rulebook.

For new players to Empire of the Sun, this one is recommended to start with when learning the game. Second would be the 1943 scenario (17.3) and the last is the 1942 Shortened Campaign (17.8).

Small introductory scenario, uses the Burma mini-map, four game turns long.

This scenario examines Burma, the lowest priority Allied theater. The scenario begins on turn 6 with each side receiving a reduced hand of cards. On turns 7, 8, and 9, each side begins with a base of four cards that can be reduced for both sides by a variety of reasons specific to a particular session of the scenario.
